## 1. English — Plain Language Privacy Notice

### Who We Are
OKOA AI is an anonymous, 24/7 digital mental health and addiction recovery companion accessible via WhatsApp, designed primarily for Kenyan youth (ages 15–35). Our mission is to provide stigma-free, non-judgmental support, relapse prevention, and direct connections to verified rehabilitation and youth empowerment centers.

### Our Core Privacy Promise: Zero Identifiable Footprint
1. **No Real Names:** You never provide your name or national ID.
2. **Encrypted Vault Isolation:** Your WhatsApp phone number is isolated in an AES-256-GCM encrypted cryptographic vault. Our AI conversation engine never sees, receives, or logs your phone number. You are identified across the system solely by an anonymous random UUID (e.g., `4a7b9c1d-...`).
3. **No GPS Tracking:** We never capture or request GPS coordinates. When you search for nearby rehabilitation centers, we collect only coarse regional selection (County or Sub-County level).
4. **No Medical Advice:** The AI does not diagnose illnesses or prescribe medication. In crises, you are immediately routed to human crisis lines (such as **1199** or **1192**).

### Your Legal Rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019)
Under Sections 26, 30, and 40 of the Act, you hold absolute rights over your data:
- **Right to Access (§ 26):** Reply **`MAELEZO`** or **`DATA SUMMARY`** to view a summary of the data we hold about your session.
- **Right to Erasure / The "Right to be Forgotten" (§ 40):** Reply **`FUTA DATA YANGU`** or **`DELETE MY DATA`**. We execute an immediate, irreversible hard delete of your conversations, mood entries, recovery streaks, and tombstone your encrypted identity.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent / Opt-Out (§ 30):** Reply **`STOP`** or **`FUTA`** to pause all automated check-ins and communications.
- **Right to Complain:** You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Commissioner:
  * Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)
  * Website: `www.odpc.go.ke` | Email: `complaints@odpc.go.ke`

## 2. Kiswahili — Ilani ya Kulinda Data na Faragha

### Sisi ni Nani?
OKOA AI ni mshirika wa kidijitali wa afya ya akili na kusaidia kupona kutokana na uraibu unaopatikana kupitia WhatsApp saa 24/7 bila unyanyapaa.

### Ahadi Yetu Kuu ya Faragha
1. **Hakuna Majina:** Huhitaji kutoa jina lako halisi wala kitambulisho cha kitaifa.
2. **Chumba Salama cha Siri (Encrypted Vault):** Nambari yako ya WhatsApp imefungwa kwa ulinzi mkali wa AES-256. Akili Mnemba (AI) haioni wala haihifadhi namba yako. Tunakutambua kwa msimbo maalum wa herufi (UUID) pekee.
3. **Hakuna GPS:** Hatuchukui eneo lako kamwe. Unapotafuta kituo cha msaada, unachagua kaunti yako pekee.

### Haki Zako Kisheria (DPA 2019)
- **Kufuta Data Yako Yote (Kifungu cha 40):** Andika **`FUTA DATA YANGU`** kufuta mazungumzo na rekodi zote kabisa.
- **Kupata Maelezo ya Data Yako (Kifungu cha 26):** Andika **`MAELEZO`** kuona muhtasari wa rekodi zako.
- **Kusimamisha Ujumbe (Kifungu cha 30):** Andika **`STOP`** au **`FUTA`**.

## 3. Sheng — Mwongozo wa Faragha kwa Vijana

### Rada Yetu ni Gani?
OKOA AI iko hapa kukubamba na kukuinua bila kukujudge. Iwe ni msongo wa mawazo au unadai kuachana na vileo, tuko WhatsApp 24/7.

### Vile Tunalinda Siri Yako (Zero Snitching)
1. **Hakuna Majina:** Hatudai jina yako, kitambulisho, wala stori zako za mtaani.
2. **Namba Yako Iko Chini ya Maji:** Namba yako ya WhatsApp inafungwa na encryption ya AES-256 kwa vault salama. AI yetu inakuita tu na UUID anonymous.
3. **GPS Hatuna:** Hatutaki kujua mtaa wako specific. Ukidai rehab au empowerment center, unachagua tu County.
4. **Ukitaka Kufuta Kila Kitu:** Andika tu **`FUTA DATA YANGU`** na system inawipe kila trace yako mara moja bila maswali.
